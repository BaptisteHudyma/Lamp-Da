void set_brightness_level (const uint8_t level)
 Set the brightness of the RGB indicator. Indicator level can be 0 : indicator and alerts displayed at full brightness 1 : indicator and alerts displayed at low brightness 2 : indicator and alerts displayed at low brightness, indicator off when charging After a level update, the normal indicator stays visible for 5 seconds, even while charging at level 2.
