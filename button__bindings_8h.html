Detailed Description

User configurable links between click counts and default behaviors.

The click action (NC) and the hold action (NH) of a behavior always move together, so that a binding change cannot split a behavior between two click counts. Two behaviors can share a click count if they do not use the same action, like the quick navigation (click only) and the scroll (hold only).

Click counts 1 and 2 (on/off, next mode, brightness) and 10 and above (shutdown, resets) cannot be configured. 3 clicks is always the group navigation, so only the behaviors without a click action can use 3 clicks.
