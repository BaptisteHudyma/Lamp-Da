static hal::mutex::hal_mutex_t _raiseTimeMutex
 Guards the raise times of all alerts (AlertBase::raisedTime): refreshed by raise() from any thread (power thread, BLE, ...), while the main loop takes its timeout decisions on them. The elapsed times are computed under the lock, so a refresh can never be seen as "in the future" of the decision. Not recursive.
