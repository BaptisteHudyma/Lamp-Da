◆ _isSinkingToSwap

bool lampda::logic::power::_isSinkingToSwap = false
static

attached (or attaching) as a sink, to ask for a swap

Battery mode, partner powering VBUS (issue #357). A dual role partner with a 3A pull-up to 3.3V (4.7k, laptops) reads our Rp in parallel with the board permanent Rd under its Rd threshold: it takes the lamp for a sink, and powers VBUS. No CC setting can fix it (the board Rd is hardware), so the lamp attaches as a sink, without using that power, and asks for the source role (power role swap).
