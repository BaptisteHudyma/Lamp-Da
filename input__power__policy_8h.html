 True when the VBUS measure can come from our own OTG output, and is not a power source The charger OTG also powers the output gate (output mode), and the two gates are never enabled together: the USB port is then isolated from our output, and VBUS can only come from an external source. It must be followed, or a cable unplugged in output mode is never seen (issue #646). While the gates are switching (both disabled), the port can still hold our own voltage: it is ignored too.
