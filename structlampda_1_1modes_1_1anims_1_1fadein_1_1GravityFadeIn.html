Detailed Description

template<size_t MaxParticles = 20>
struct lampda::modes::anims::fadein::GravityFadeIn< MaxParticles >

Reveal an animation with falling pixels.

The whole lamp starts masked. Particles spawn at the top, fall, and unmask the pixel they land on. Landing targets are picked line by line from the bottom to the top (random inside the line), so the reveal rises uniformly: no empty column, no clump higher than one line. The spawn rate follows the (extrapolated) progress, and the lamp is fully unmasked when progress reaches 1.

Parameters
MaxParticlesMaximum number of particles falling at the same time (throughput limit)
