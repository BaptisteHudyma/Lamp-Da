◆ fullLevel

constexpr uint16_t lampda::component::charger::policy::fullLevel = 9900
staticconstexpr

Battery level (percent * 100) from which the battery is considered full.

The charging IC regulates the pack voltage on its own reference (+-0.5%, ~60mV at 12.3V) and does not see the cells: it can settle above the target. The charge is stopped from the balancer measurements, that are more precise.
